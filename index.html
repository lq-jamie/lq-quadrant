The Strategic Picture

Beachhead: ICP-A (top-right). These clients move fast, have budget, and are already asking the right questions. The Board Demo Sprint is the entry point — low commitment, real artifact, immediate board-level win. Champion Accelerator is the follow-on. Everything else requires trust that hasn't been earned yet.

Volume play: ICP-E (bottom-right). Solo GCs make fast decisions and pay for consumption. Oscar-style delivery works here with minimal trust-building overhead. Low budget but low friction.

Long game: ICP-B and ICP-D (top-left). High budget but skeptical. The Tool-Limit Push earns trust by working inside their existing stack and demonstrating the gap. Slow, but meaningful when they convert.

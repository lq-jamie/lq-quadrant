The Strategic Picture

LQ's real moat is the trust network. Harvey, Norm, and every other legal AI vendor can't buy what you've built in this community — years of shared reputation, personal relationships, WhatsApp conversations, and hackathon credibility. That trust map is the asymmetric advantage.

Beachhead: top-right. ICP-A, C, E with high LQ trust are your warm leads. They already know you. Board Demo Sprint is the natural first engagement — low risk, real artifact, immediate win. Champion Accelerator, Oscar-style, and Co-Design follow as trust deepens.

Expansion: top-left. ICP-B and ICP-D have budget but no LQ trust yet. Board Demo Sprint plant the seed — it's low-commitment enough to get in the door. Then you earn trust from inside their environment before pitching anything bigger.

Network effect: bottom-right. Community followers, digest readers, LQ members at other firms — these are your referral nodes. Hackathons, Open Cohort, and Async Sprints expand the trust map outward. Every engaged community member is a future champion or referral channel.

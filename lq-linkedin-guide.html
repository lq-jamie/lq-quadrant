LQ on LinkedIn — Practical Guide

Where to list LegalQuants. What to say. What to avoid.

LegalQuants is not an employer. It's a community of legal technologists who build things together. That distinction matters for how you represent it on professional profiles — and whether doing so creates professional risk.

Where to list it

Recommended: Organisations
Best fit
Organisations is designed for affiliations, not employment. It reads as "this is a group I belong to" — accurate and low-implication. Use this unless you're unsure about everything else.
Situational: About Me / Summary
Caution
Mentioning LQ in your About Me is natural and creates context. Safe for independent practitioners. Less safe if you're employed and want to keep your side activities off your employer's radar.
Avoid: Experience / Volunteering
Avoid
Experience implies a formal role or employment. Listing LQ as a job or project role can trigger questions about conflicts of interest with your current employer.
Avoid
Volunteering implies charitable activity, which can undersell what LQ actually is — a serious professional community producing real tools and commercial outcomes.
The employment risk
If you're employed at a law firm or in-house legal team, your employer may take an interest in activities that could affect them — especially if LQ is perceived as competing with their services or representing a conflict of interest. The safe default: keep your LQ affiliation in Organisations until you know your employer's stance. This is especially true for practitioners at large law firms or established legal tech companies.
